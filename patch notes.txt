2.90

NEW

Eidolon (Herald) support
The performance changes now cover the Eidolon as well:
- Strikecraft squadrons reduced to 1 ship - their weapons hit harder to compensate.
- Carrier Cruiser carries 1 squadron (slightly longer build time), hangar module +1.
- Fewer trade and traffic ships (1 per port, longer build time).
- Squadron research now costs exotics.

Advent Sisters support
- The AI builds the Sisters' own defenses in sensible numbers (beam defense max 15 per planet, Temple of Renewal max 10).
- Load this mod AFTER Advent Sisters if you use both.


CHANGED

AI building limits (all factions)
- The AI builds at most 15 turrets of each type per planet. Large AI empires no longer flood planets with hundreds of turrets - a big gain on large maps.
- The AI builds healing structures in military slots in moderation (1 per 3 used slots, max 10).

Balance
- Hangar defense price back to RRR values.


2.89

General / Bug Fixes

Updated for game version 2.1.1 and RRR 5.8.3-A.
Fixed game start errors (Vasari cloak research subjects not found, missing effect alias definitions on Trader Robotics Cruiser and Vasari Overseer Cruiser).
The mod is now built on top of the current RRR / game files and only overrides the values it actually changes (216 files instead of 409). All new RRR and game content (corruption, shroud, Eidolon interaction, new research, sounds and effects) is now fully available with this mod enabled.


Performance

Unchanged: debris despawn after 5 seconds, strikecraft squadron size 1, reduced carrier / starbase / hangar defense squadron capacity, reduced trade and traffic ships, strikecraft weapons with doubled cooldown and damage, siege capital missile barrages with fewer but stronger missiles.


Balance

The following values now follow RRR again instead of older copies kept by this mod:
Advent Starbase armor, Missile Frigate damage, Vasari Antifighter Frigate missile range, research time of Carrier Cruiser unlock and Missile Targeting, faction research / trade / culture modifiers.
Missile weapons now use RRR's own target acquisition (order target only).
