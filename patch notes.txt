2.8

General / Bug Fixes

Not save compatible.


Performance

All debris pieces across every faction (ships, starbases, titans, generic wreckage) now despawn after 5 seconds instead of 240-900 seconds depending on debris type. Massively reduces the number of simulated debris objects in long games.
Trade ship construction time at Trader Starbases increased 1200 -> 1800 (20 -> 30 min), reducing the number of trade ships that accumulate over time.
Missile Corvette main weapon no longer auto-acquires the best target in range mid-flight (acquire_target_logic: order_target_or_best_target_in_range -> order_target_only), cutting down on retargeting overhead.
Hangar Defense Structures (Advent + Trader, Loyalist/Rebel) squadron capacity reduced from 4-6 -> 1, cutting simulated strikecraft counts around static defenses.
Trader Colony Capital Ship squadron capacity roughly halved across all 20 experience levels (e.g. max level 8 -> 6), reducing strikecraft load from leveled colony ships.


Balance

Missile weapons reworked across most factions: cooldown and per-hit damage halved (DPS-neutral) for the majority of swarm/torpedo weapons, while torpedo flight/firing duration increased (typically 12s -> 15-30s depending on weapon).

Squadron Size research (Tier 0-1) now also affects Capital Ships and Starbases, not just Super Capital Ships and Titans.
Squadron Size Tier 2 completely reworked: now costs 8000 Credits / 3000 Metal / 3000 Crystal + 4 Research Exotics (was free) and trades squadron count for damage (-33% squadron size, +33% Fighter/Bomber weapon damage) instead of a flat strikecraft bonus.
Squadron Size Tier 3 now costs 10000 Credits / 3750 Metal / 3750 Crystal + 5 Research Exotics and actually grants its bonus (was free but non-functional).

Garrison research (Garrison Tier 0-2, Garrison Max Supply, Loyalist Garrison Max Supply, Garrison Recruitment Center unlock) effectively disabled: prices set to 999999. Garrison Tier 0-2 planet population bonus increased 10 -> 40 for when re-enabled. Garrison Recruitment Center unit items removed.


Visuals

Restored exhaust particle/trail effects on ~60 ship skins (Advent, Trader, Vasari, Pirate) that previously had them stripped for performance.


Removed

Removed several minor faction starbase overrides (Aluxian, Jiskun, Pranast, Viturak) and associated unit skins/garrison items no longer used by this mod version.
